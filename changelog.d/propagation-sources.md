### Changed

- Planting: the season plan now names its sources — the RHS, the USDA Woody
  Plant Seed Manual and Audubon — and each technique page links to further
  reading. Seed tasks on plants birds eat from say to leave some standing.
- Planting: trees and shrubs no longer get seed reminders. They first bear seed
  anywhere from 3 to 40 years old, so the plan doesn't guess. Their cuttings
  and suckers still show.
- Internal: the old 5-year rule for trees was wrong — the Woody Plant Seed
  Manual puts white oak at 20. `GUIDE_REFS` in `lib/planting.ts` keeps each
  source's checked quote beside its link.
