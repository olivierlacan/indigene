### Added

- Internal: `npm run harvest:fnps` reads the Florida Native Plant Society's plant pages (nursery availability, light, moisture, soil, zones) as a shortlist for both Florida regions, slowly and within their robots.txt. Its pages count as a native-status claim that the Atlas and Kew must corroborate; nursery availability never does. `data/sources/atlas-florida/` documents the Atlas of Florida Plants county search a person runs by hand, since its robots.txt keeps scripts off.
