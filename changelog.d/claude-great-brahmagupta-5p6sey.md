### Added

- Internal: `npm run pnw:checklist` reads the Flora of the Pacific Northwest Checklist (Burke Museum, University of Washington) and OregonFlora's checklist (Oregon State University, downloaded by hand) — every plant, native and introduced — and joins them to herbarium specimens from the 37 counties west of the Cascade crest. Committed facts-only under `data/sources/pnw-flora/`. Seven of our 106 PNW rows are flagged for a person, five of them names.
