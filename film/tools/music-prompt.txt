Original instrumental score for a 60-second hand-drawn animated nature explainer about native plants, birds and caterpillars. Warm, hopeful, intimate, humanistic — like a Studio Ghibli / Laika-style indie short or a gentle nature documentary. Instrumentation: fingerpicked nylon-string acoustic guitar, felt piano, soft pizzicato strings, light glockenspiel/celesta sparkles, warm cello pad, very subtle brushed percussion appearing later. No vocals, no lyrics.
Structure (tempo about 84 BPM, key of D major):
0-5s: sparse and slightly wistful — solo felt piano, a few quiet notes with space (a quiet, lifeless lawn).
5-16s: gentle curiosity — pizzicato and guitar enter, playful but light (caterpillars and birds).
16-33s: a warm steady pulse builds — guitar arpeggios, cello, soft glockenspiel; a sense of discovery and measuring (sun, soil, climate; plants growing).
33-45s: fullest and most tender — strings swell gently, brushed percussion, a hummable melody (people of all ages gardening together).
45-53s: uplifting resolution, blooming, hopeful.
53-60s: soft final cadence and a gentle ring-out ending on the tonic, fully resolved, decaying naturally to silence.
Keep it understated and mixed as a bed under narration: no loud hits, no drops, no heavy drums, mid-range kept clear for a voice.
