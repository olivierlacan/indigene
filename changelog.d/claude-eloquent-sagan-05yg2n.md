### Added

- **[Your first planting day](https://indigene.app/volunteer): four short guides to read before a volunteer planting.** Planting from a pot, what to do with invasives you pull, digging among tree roots, and what to bring. Organizers can send the link ahead.
- Planting: every tip in the [planting-day guides](https://indigene.app/volunteer/roots) was checked against two published sources, and where gardeners disagree, as on washing roots bare, the page says so.
- Internal: `lib/volunteer.ts` holds the guides' sections and sources; `volunteer.test.ts` fails a section cited by fewer than two publishers. Figures are a third family in `page-art-svg.mjs`, labelled rather than hidden.
