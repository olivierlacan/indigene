### Added

- Regions: 65 more natives to plant this fall and winter — trees that go in bare, bulbs for autumn, seed that wants a winter outdoors. The [Pacific Northwest](https://indigene.app/regions/pnw), both Floridas, the Scottish Lowlands and the St Lawrence Lowlands each gain 11 to 14.
- A few to look for: chocolate lily, highbush blueberry, bald cypress, wood anemone and tamarack. In Florida, the cool, dry months are the planting season, and the new rows say so.
- Internal: picked from fresh `npm run candidates` shortlists in the five regions with a second checklist (`SECOND_CHECKS`), weighted to woody plants, autumn bulbs and fall-sown seed. Every row is native per Kew (`native:check`) and per the region's own checklist: Burke + OregonFlora, the Atlas of Florida Plants, BSBI (330–414 hectads), VASCAN. `candidates.mjs` now ranks Florida rows only when the Atlas agrees. Left out: ashes, elms, butternut (disease), orchids and mycoheterotrophs, seagrasses and epiphytes.
