### Changed

- Plants: every region's "How to grow more" notes were checked against the
  guides they cite, and rewritten where those guides disagree — coast
  redwood does grow from seed, bald cypress does need a cold spell, and
  pōhutukawa seed is sown fresh, not dried.
- Plants: a note now names only sources we opened and found saying the same
  thing. Where none could be found, the note keeps one plain line and says
  "No published source checked yet."
- Internal: ten regions' propagation rows audited by hand against the Woody
  Plant Seed Manual, NRCS plant guides, FEIS, Native Plant Network
  protocols, NZPCN, FNPS, RHS and Seed Information Database records. Book
  and society citations nobody could open (Metcalf, Theodore Payne, the
  Conservatoires botaniques) no longer back a note. New linked sources:
  FEIS, NRCS plant guides and fact sheets, UC Master Gardeners.
