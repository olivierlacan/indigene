### Added

- Regions: 262 more native plants, so every region with two native checks now offers at least 80. [Auckland](https://indigene.app/regions/nz-auckland) grows most, from 47 to 82, with nīkau, akeake and the rātā vines akatea and akakura.
- Continental France, the French Alps and Atlantic France roughly double, to 81 each, in English and French: beech, silver fir, arnica, martagon lily, cowslip, pasque flower, meadowsweet and more.
- Short lists filled where they were thin: ferns and grasses in Northern Michigan, shrubs and vines in the Mid-Atlantic, wildflowers in both Floridas, groundcovers in Chicago. Each is native per Kew and per the region's own botanists.
- Internal: per-form floors for the thirteen `SECOND_CHECKS` regions (tree 14, shrub 16, perennial 22, grass 6, groundcover 6, fern 5, vine 4; Auckland's own). Scotland stays two trees short: BSBI calls the rest introduced there. Three Illinois synonyms join `REVIEWED` in `check-floras.mjs`. New French names checked against the TAXREF v18 extract. Reconcile kept to new taxa; its iNaturalist taxon swaps for 17 existing plants are left for a separate change.
