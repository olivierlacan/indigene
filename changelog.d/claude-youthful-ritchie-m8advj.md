### Changed
- **Seven icons look like what they stand for.** The bee, moth, butterfly,
  squirrel, tortoise, shrub and wildflower are redrawn from photographs, so you
  can tell them apart even at text size. They sit beside each
  [wildlife group](https://indigene.app/wildlife) and wherever a photo is missing.
- Wildlife & Plants: the pictures that appear when you share a link now show the
  new icons and today's numbers. Some were weeks out of date — the goldfinch's
  card said 14 native plants; it's 28.
- Internal: shrub and perennial are generated by `scripts/gen-form-glyphs.mjs`
  (its unused `dome()` helper is gone); the five animals are hand-set in
  `wildlife-glyphs.ts`. All share cards under `public/og/` that draw a glyph
  (plants, wildlife, alternatives, invasives, look-alikes) are regenerated;
  rendering is deterministic, so an unchanged card comes back byte-identical.
